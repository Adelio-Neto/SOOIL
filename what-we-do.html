Our expertise

We combine banking expertise, private equity and a deep knowledge of Angola's energy sector to finance exploration and production.

From opportunity analysis to structured capital, we support each stage of producing oil fields in the Angolan Oil & Gas industry.

Oil & Gas value chain

Creating value at every stage of the production chain

Exploration

Financing the discovery and appraisal of new fields.

Exploration

Capital for discovering and appraising new fields

We finance exploration and appraisal programmes, covering seismic studies, drilling campaigns and independent reserve certification.

Our analysts structure investments around technical de-risking milestones, so capital reaches value as risk is reduced.

Development

Structured capital for bringing fields into production.

Development

Structured capital to bring fields into production

We provide equity and quasi-equity solutions for the development phase, from facilities construction to first oil.

Debt raising and project finance complement our capital, aligning maturity with each asset's ramp-up curve.

Production

Value creation through assets in full operation.

Production

Creating value from assets in full operation

For producing fields, we invest in optimisation, workovers and efficiency measures that increase recovery and extend economic life.

We work alongside operators, from analysis to follow-up, turning assets into lasting value.

Project Financing

Customised capital structures for long-term projects.

Project Financing

Capital structured around each project's cash flow

From debt raising to syndication with international partners, we design financing solutions that match the risk and returns of each producing asset.

We combine project finance discipline with the flexibility of private equity to support long-term value creation.
